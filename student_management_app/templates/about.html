About the application

Amresh Student Management System

Sign in

A Django application for coordinating student, staff, course, attendance, and academic-result workflows.

The system provides separate workspaces for administrators, staff, and students. Depending on their account, users can manage institutional records, take or review attendance, work with results, submit leave requests, and exchange feedback.
